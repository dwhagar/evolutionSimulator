# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here, newest first, grouped by `MAJOR.minor` line. The build
number changes with every merge, so entries reference the line rather than each build. Each entry
carries the task ID(s) from [TODO.md](TODO.md) and links the PR. See [docs/versioning.md](docs/versioning.md).

## 0.1 — Data models for DNA and traits

### Added
- `REPRO.8`, `DNA.9`, `TRAIT.7`: The revised reproduction genes: drive, cooldown, fertility (litter and gestation), parenting, recovery and a sex digit; sexed mating, pregnancy kept on the bearer, births paid by both parents, healing at an energy cost, and wear, upkeep and timers charged per reference hour (animal layout version 5). A long-run check script shows no gene piling up at an end. [#101](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/101)
- `BRAIN.22`: Pain, a new threat or a new contact interrupts the decision interval, so a creature with a slow reaction time still reacts at once to harm. [#108](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/108)
- `BRAIN.9`: Seeded brains: the first generation gets a small brain fitted to the old fixed rule (95%+ agreement, tested), with a regeneration script, so founders start with sensible behaviour instead of random wiring. Founders still die out before breeding in the default world (`WORLD.15`). [#106](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/106)
- `SENSE.16`: Sense genes: `sight_fov` as a half-angle (15 to 180 degrees), a `sight_acuity` gene with the hard trade-off `min(acuity, 1 - field)`, acuity-based reach, noise and cost, and first generations seeded at mid values with a diet tilt, so founders are no longer born blind (animal layout version 6). [#103](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/103)
- `WORLD.13`: Faster ticks: sensing only on ticks when the brain will run, neighbours found by surface in the spatial index, and each neighbour measured once in `sense()`; about 69 to 44 ms per tick at 300 animals and 3000 plants. [#99](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/99)
- `SENSE.12`: The world now senses through the real sensing step (`WorldSenses`): neighbour search and classifier with each animal's real organs, daylight and the world edge, and loud alarm calls waking sleepers. Calls are off by default until the call rule exists. [#97](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/97)
- `SENSE.15`: Design note on the sense genes (`docs/design/sense-genes.md`): a field of vision as a half-angle, a `sight_acuity` gene with a hard trade-off, predator and prey defaults, and what the world must provide for each sense; owner questions [#88](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/88) to [#92](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/92) (`SENSE.17` to `SENSE.21`); the builds are `SENSE.16` and `WORLD.12`. [#95](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/95)
- `BRAIN.24`: Learning during life: curiosity noise, a day log with a delayed reward, and a nightly update of the output layer on falling asleep, kept as offsets on the creature and not inherited. [#93](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/93)
- `BRAIN.23`, `SENSE.9`: The evolving brain shape: a gated 16-slot brain with a dead zone, operating genes (`brain_gain`, `reaction_time`, `curiosity`, `learning_rate`), and the three new inputs (pain, daylight, relative size), at animal layout version 4. [#85](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/85)
- `REPRO.3` (plant part): Plants are asexual with a mutated clone, slower mutation, `seed_provision` and `seeding_interval` genes, a seed count paid from the seed budget, and a lifespan (plant layout version 2). [#81](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/81)
- `DNA.14`: Design note on traits that read clusters of genes (`docs/design/gene-clusters.md`); owner questions 7 and 8 added to [#69](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/69); the build is `DNA.15`. [#80](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/80)
- `WORLD.8`: Plants limit each other's soil and light, so a plant population settles below the cap. [#79](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/79)
- `REPRO.7`: Reproduction genes design revised to the owner's answers (`docs/design/reproduction-genes.md`): drive, cooldown, fertility (litter and gestation), parenting, a recovery gene, plant longevity, absolute durations; the build is `REPRO.8`. [#77](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/77)
- `REPRO.4`: Animals prefer the mate with the closest genome. [#76](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/76)
- `REPRO.5`, `TRAIT.6`: Reproduction genes `caregiving`, `repro_interval` and `repro_drive` replace `fertility` (animal layout version 3), births are paid by both parents or not at all (`give_birth`), and more repair makes births dearer and the interval longer, with an upkeep cost that grows with the square. The world takes child energy and seed biomass from the parents. [#74](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/74)
- `WORLD.5`: Day-night cycle and sleep: daylight from simulated time, darker nights shrink vision, sleeping animals stop sensing, moving, eating and mating and burn less energy, with a sleep listener hook for nightly learning. [#70](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/70)
- `SENSE.11`: Sense genes: ranges that reach zero (a sense can be lost), noise that falls with the gene, and a cost charged on the area covered. [#68](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/68)
- `WORLD.9`: The sensing and brain pass runs about 2.7 times faster. [#67](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/67)
- `SENSE.10`: The sensing step as a pure function (`sense()`), with its constants in `SensingRules`. [#65](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/65)
- `BRAIN.21`: One threshold (`Brain(dead_zone=...)`) for the brain's energy cost and its decisions. [#72](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/72)
- `TRAIT.5`: Start-of-life state set at construction and a mating-cost guard against negative energy. [#66](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/66)
- `SENSE.8`: Sense-channel registry: one table of brain inputs that generates `INPUT_NAMES`, `Percepts` and `build_inputs`. [#61](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/61)
- `DOCS.7`: Decisions log updated with the owner's answers to the clarification issues [#36](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/36) and [#27](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/27) (closed); the tasks they changed are `DNA.14`, `REPRO.7`, `TRAIT.7`, `TRAIT.8`, `WORLD.7`, with three points still unclear in [#69](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/69).
- `DNA.13`: Diploid genomes: `DiploidDNA` with a per-gene `DominanceModel` (default codominant average), gametes from each parent, and a `Genome` protocol so traits accept either kind. Haploid stays plain `DNA`. [#63](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/63)
- `BRAIN.13`, `BRAIN.15`: Design notes on a brain shape that evolves inside a fixed-length genome (`docs/design/brain-shape.md`) and on learning during life (`docs/design/brain-learning.md`); owner questions [#52](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/52) to [#56](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/56) and [#57](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/57) filed (`BRAIN.17` to `BRAIN.20`, `WORLD.6`). The builds are `BRAIN.23` and `BRAIN.24`. [#60](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/60)
- `WORLD.2`, `WORLD.3`, `WORLD.4`: The world. Continuous 2D space with user-set bounds (default 1000 by 1000, walls at the edges), a spatial index for neighbours, stand-in senses, the step loop, and time scales with all the presets and a pacer that caps ticks per call (`evolution_simulator.world`, `docs/design/world.md`). [#53](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/53)
- `DNA.8`: Mutation favours low digits (`position_bias`, default 0.5, plants 0.75) with a separate `locality` setting for how random a change is (default fully random) and per-gene biases from `TraitDef.mutation_step`. [#58](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/58)
- `REPRO.6`: Design note on the reproduction genes (caregiving, reproduction interval, reproductive drive) and the longevity trade-off (`docs/design/reproduction-genes.md`); owner questions 7 to 11 added to [#36](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/36). The build is `REPRO.5` and `TRAIT.6`. [#49](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/49)
- `BRAIN.11`: Decision interval: the brain runs every n-th `think` (`decision_interval`, default 1) and its energy cost per tick is divided by n. [#50](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/50)
- `DNA.7`: Crossover now defaults to `DNA.crossover_linked`: a few cut points that follow whole genes and brain neurons, with `gene_fidelity` and `mean_cuts` settings. [#48](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/48)
- `BRAIN.14`: Behaviour genes (aggression, fear) bend what the brain perceives through a `Temperament` in the brain adapter. [#47](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/47)
- `SENSE.1`: Design note on senses and how they feed the brain (`docs/design/senses.md`); owner questions filed as [#40](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/40) to [#45](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/45). [#46](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/46)
- `BRAIN.16`: Random wander fallback for creatures that cannot decide. [#39](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/39)
- `DOCS.6`: Decisions log (`docs/decisions.md`) recording the owner's answers to the first round of issues and the tasks they created. Open clarifications are in [#36](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/36).
- `BRAIN.2`, `DNA.11`, `DNA.12`: The animal brain. A pure 17-8-6 feedforward `Brain` (198 weights) with an adapter from percepts to decisions, a `BlockSpec` gene block for weight arrays stored in the DNA (the animal genome is now 461 digits, layout version 2), per-segment expected-change mutation, whole-neuron crossover (`crossover_by_unit`), and brain upkeep added to basal metabolism. `AnimalTraits.think()` returns the decision; acting on it is not built yet. [#34](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/34)
- `DOCS.5`: Document on how DNA and traits interact (`docs/design/dna-traits-interaction.md`). [#32](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/32)
- `TRAIT.4`, `DNA.10`: Traits as data. A genome layout (`GenomeLayout`, `GeneSpec`) lists the genes by ID, with a name and version that saved genomes check on load; trait definitions (`TraitDef`, `TraitSet`) say how each trait reads genes. DNA is now one generic class and no longer knows trait names. [#31](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/31)
- `BRAIN.1`: Design note on a small feedforward neural network brain for animals (`docs/design/brain.md`): 17 inputs, 8 hidden neurons, 6 outputs, weights stored in the DNA, with six owner decisions filed as issues ([#23](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/23) to [#28](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/issues/28), `BRAIN.3` to `BRAIN.8`). [#29](https://github.com/dwhagar/evolutionSimulator/pull/29)
